Given the Dell Inspiron board's standard 30-pin eDP output, I'd suggest:

Primary screen (top half like a 3DS): a 7.9" or 8" panel via HDMI using the board's USB-C/HDMI port — much easier, tons of options
If you want direct eDP: go 11.6" 1080p IPS eDP 30-pin (~$25–40 on Amazon/AliExpress) — guaranteed compatibility, just larger than 6"
Avoid trying to drive a phone-sourced MIPI DSI panel — those require a separate controller and won't work with the laptop's eDP output at all
